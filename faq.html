Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you order.

Do you deliver islandwide?

Yes. We deliver across Sri Lanka. Final delivery details are confirmed during order review.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Cash on delivery and bank transfer support are available where suitable. Card payment activation is being prepared through approved gateways.

Are prices final?

We confirm supplier price and availability before accepting payment, so your order remains accurate.

Can you help me choose products?

Yes. Message us with your skin type, concern, current routine, and budget for a routine suggestion.

Can I order through WhatsApp or Instagram?

Yes. Send product names or screenshots and we will create your order manually.
