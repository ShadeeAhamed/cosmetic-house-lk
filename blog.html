Routine-first beauty tips

Simple, elegant guidance for skincare, hair care, SPF, and trending beauty routines in Sri Lanka.

Beginner skincare routine

Start with cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen. Add actives slowly after patch testing.

How to layer serums

Use lightweight hydrating serums before cream textures, and keep retinol for night routines.

SPF in Sri Lanka

Daily sunscreen is a core morning step, especially when using brightening or exfoliating products.

Barrier care

If skin feels tight or irritated, pause strong actives and focus on hydration and moisturizers.

Hair care reset

Use shampoo for scalp, conditioner for lengths, and a weekly mask when hair feels dry.

Smart shopping

Confirm price and availability before payment so your order stays smooth and clear.
