Payment care

Refund Policy

Cosmetic House LK reviews every refund request carefully so customers can order with confidence and clear expectations.

When refunds are considered

Refunds are considered only for approved cases such as incorrect items, damaged items, expired items, or an order that cannot be fulfilled after confirmation.

Refund method

Refunds will only be processed back to the original payment method/payment-initiated media used during the transaction.

Review process

Customers must contact us within 24 hours of receiving an order with the order ID, product photos, parcel photos, and a clear explanation. Approved refunds are processed after review.

Non-refundable items

Opened, used, swatched, unsealed, or customer-damaged cosmetic and skincare products are not eligible for refund due to hygiene and safety reasons.

Processing time

Processing time may vary depending on the original payment provider, bank, or payment network.
