## Watermark rule

- Do not keep supplier, website, Pinterest, or third-party watermarks.
- Use only one small Cosmetic House.lk logo/watermark.
- Place it in a corner or on a subtle pill label.
- Keep it small, premium, and matched to the post colors.
- For dark posts: use white/soft pink logo treatment.
- For light posts: use black/magenta logo treatment.
- Do not cover product labels or important product details.
- Working product images in `assets/products` now already include the small Cosmetic House.lk watermark.
- Clean originals are backed up in `assets/products-original-before-watermark`.

## Design style

- Premium black, white, blush pink, and magenta accent.
- Clean spacing and strong product focus.
- Use real approved product photos.
- Avoid clutter, cartoon effects, heavy gradients, and fake luxury noise.
- Keep text short enough for mobile.

## Canva workflow

1. Open the saved Canva template.
2. Duplicate the page/design for the day.
3. Replace the product photo with the approved image from the calendar.
4. Remove any non-Cosmetic House watermark from the image.
5. Add only the small Cosmetic House.lk watermark.
6. Paste the caption from the Business Suite calendar.
7. Export or schedule through Meta Business Suite.
